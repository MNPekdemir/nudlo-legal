Support

Questions about tasks, permissions, reminders or purchases? Get in touch.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Email mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the issue. Include only the information needed to investigate.

A reminder did not appear

Check that notifications are allowed for Nudlo and review Focus and notification-summary settings. For a location or walking reminder, also check the required location and Motion & Fitness permissions. Location verification can delay a reminder, and step updates may stop while iOS suspends the app.

Walking and leaving a place are different

A walking reminder reacts to newly detected steps during its active tracking window. Walking indoors can count. A location reminder reacts to entering or leaving a selected area and does not require the same short walking-tracking window. Neither method guarantees a notification at the exact first step or door threshold.

Your local data

Tasks and reminder settings are stored on your device. We cannot view or restore that content remotely. For details on deletion, device backups and permissions, read the Privacy Policy.

Purchase support

If paid features are available in your installed version, use the Apple Account that made the purchase when restoring access. Apple handles its App Store billing and refund requests. Contact us if the app does not recognize your entitlement; do not email payment-card details.
