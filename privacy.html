Privacy Policy

How the local-first version of Nudlo handles your information.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your tasks, saved places and movement information stay in the app on your device. Nudlo does not operate an app-data server, use advertising SDKs or run third-party app analytics.

Who is responsible

Nudlo is provided by MNPekdemir. For privacy questions or requests, contact mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com. This policy covers the local-first app and this legal/support website.

Tasks and settings

The app stores the tasks you enter, completion status, reminder rules, saved places and appearance settings locally so it can show your tasks and manage reminders. We do not receive this content from the app. The app does not require a Nudlo account.

Location and movement permissions

Location is used when you choose a location reminder or enable an active walking reminder. The app may use precise location and saved coordinates to determine whether a place has been entered or left. Walking reminders use motion activity and step-count information to recognize walking and evaluate the reminder you configured.

These features request the relevant iOS permissions. Background access can continue while an eligible reminder or tracking session is active, subject to iOS restrictions. Active walking tracking ends when its configured window ends or its last active reminder is cancelled. A location reminder can remain registered with iOS until it fires or is cancelled. Nudlo does not send a location or walking history to us and does not use this information for advertising.

Notifications and widgets

iOS handles scheduled notifications. Task text and reminder information can appear in notifications and in widgets on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. Choose what information you want visible there; you can change notification preview settings and remove widgets in iOS.

Storage, retention and deletion

Tasks, saved places and reminder settings remain locally until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Completing a task can keep its history while cancelling the active reminder. Revoking a permission stops the related access; it does not automatically erase previously saved task or place settings.

Deleting the app normally removes its app data from that device. Offloading an app is different and can retain its data. Nudlo does not provide its own cloud synchronization in this version. Device backups controlled through Apple or your device settings may include app data, and restoring a backup may restore that data.

Your choices

You can change notification, location and Motion & Fitness permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove reminders or delete tasks in the app. Turning off a permission can prevent that feature from working. We cannot remotely view or erase content that remains exclusively on your device.

Purchases and Apple services

If paid features are offered, payments and purchase restoration are handled through Apple’s App Store. The app may process purchase entitlement information locally to determine which features are available. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may independently process device, purchase, backup or diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to attach. We use that information to answer the request, investigate the issue and meet applicable obligations. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, including any legally required retention. You can request deletion of support correspondence.

Support email is handled through Gmail and email providers. Please do not send task content, detailed location history or payment information unless it is needed to explain the issue.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies or contact forms to this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even without a GitHub login. Its handling of website traffic is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell app data or share it for targeted advertising. Support information may be processed by email providers, and website traffic by GitHub, including in countries outside your own. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. These limited support and website activities are separate from the task, place and motion information stored locally by the app.

Privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal information, object to processing, request portability or contact a data protection authority. Contact mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com about information you have provided to us. For local app content, use the app and device controls described above. We do not ask the app for your age or intentionally request children’s personal information; contact us if such information has been sent to support.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date if the app’s data handling changes. Features such as a user account, cloud synchronization or an analytics SDK require a new disclosure before they are introduced. This policy does not promise those future features.
